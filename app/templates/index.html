About Sangre Signal

Sangre Signal is an advanced stock analysis tool that examines stocks for various risk factors including:

  • Country of origin analysis
  • ADR (American Depositary Receipt) status
  • Low float detection
  • Short interest analysis
  • Institutional ownership patterns

Data sources: Yahoo Finance, FinViz
